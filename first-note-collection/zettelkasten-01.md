The idea at the heart of it is the atomic note, meaning one note per idea, written in your own words, and linked to the notes around it.

I like that the effort is front-loaded onto understanding rather than copying, because that is where the retention actually comes from.

To that point, the linking structure is doing more than organization as its connecting each note to related ones surfaces connections between ideas that I would otherwise miss at first glance.
