# Title: 

Acceptable AI Use

# Date: 

September 9th, 2026

# Source: 

Hsu, Hua. "The End of the Essay." New Yorker, July 7, 2025.

# Valuable Quote:

"He told me that he used A.I. "as a brainstorm" but never for writing itself."

# Insight:

I wholly agree with this quote and I think it's a valuable approach (especially as a student). 

As I've mentioned in my other notes, I see AI as a useful tool. AI can be an incredible brainstorming tool and study partner but we should never rely on it to write for us. 

I find it imperative that we use our voice and reason now more than ever. We have to continue to "be human" and part of our responsibility is voicing our own opinions regardless of if they are "bad or good". 

The quote is followed up by the student admitting that they used ChatGPT for quizzes when they were in a time crunch. I can sympathize with this response, only because modern universities and education in general have placed such a high-standard on getting good grades rather than a more holistic approach to learning.
