# Title: 

Cheating or Academic Dishonesty?

# Date: 

September 9th, 2026

# Source: 

Hsu, Hua. “The End of the Essay.” New Yorker, July 7, 2025.

# Valuable Quote: 

"..I noticed that he had recently asked ChatGPT whether it was O.K to go running in Nike Dunks".

# Insight:

This quote highlights an interesting dynamic between us and AI bots. Although I'm not innocent when it comes to requesting assistance with a question towards Claude or ChatGPT, it's a strange relationship. 

At this point, I find it more important to see AI as a tool that makes our life easier or more productive instead of a confidant.

Of course the example is quite innocent but I know so many people who use LLMs to ask everyday questions and most overshare personal details.
