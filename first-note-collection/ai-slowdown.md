# Title: 

Profits over Safety?

# Date: 

September 9th, 2026

# Source: 

Hu, Krystal, and Krystal Hu. “NEWSLETTER: Some Researchers Want AI to Slow down. The Labs Can’t Afford to.” Artificial Intelligence. Reuters, September 9, 2026. https://www.reuters.com/technology/artificial-intelligence/ai-models-capabilities-leap-comes-with-new-safety-warnings-2026-09-09/.

# Valuable Quote:

"The harder question is whether anyone in the race can afford to be the first to slow down."

# Insight:

To me, this quote is the perfect example of the weird crossroads we are currently at with the rapid development of AI.

It's a clear statement which supports the rest of the article's premise that companies simply aren't willing to slow down the growth even though they are aware of the risks.

Companies at this point aren't seeing enough incentive or pressure to act on the risks of the growth.
